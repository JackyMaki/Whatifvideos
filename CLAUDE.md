# Working preferences (from the repo owner)

- While a video is rendering, do not send progress text or preview images. Deliver only the
  finished MP4 (send the file), with no commentary, once it is done.
- Videos follow the house style in README.md (serif question with a red key phrase, top-left stat
  block, street-level blocky low-poly scenes, 9:16, exactly 60 s).
- Reply in plain words and short sentences.
- Narration uses Piper voice en-us-libritts-high, speaker 6 (deep, calm male), not espeak/MBROLA.
- Voice settings: length_scale 1.15, noise_scale 0.4, noise_w 0.6, flatten 0.5 (pitch range halved for a chill, even read). The owner wants a chill, monotone, non-robotic delivery.
