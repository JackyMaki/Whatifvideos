# Working preferences (from the repo owner)

- While a video is rendering, do not send progress text or preview images. Deliver only the
  finished MP4 (send the file), with no commentary, once it is done.
- Videos follow the house style in README.md (serif question with a red key phrase, top-left stat
  block, street-level blocky low-poly scenes, 9:16, exactly 60 s).
- Reply in plain words and short sentences.
- Narration uses the Kokoro neural voice am_onyx (deep, calm, natural male). Piper/espeak are fallbacks only.
- Delivery: a close-mic "loud whisper" (audio/whisper.py, mix 0.55, speed 0.95), calm and completely human. Never robotic, no exaggerated emphasis, no pitch-flattening.
- Story: every episode has a real beginning, middle and end. The character enters the world believably (walks out of a door, wakes up, arrives), each scene follows from the last, and the ending is a motion or a choice, not a freeze.
