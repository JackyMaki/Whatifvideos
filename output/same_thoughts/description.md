## Short description (for the post)

You step out of a café, think "coffee", and every head on the street thinks it too. One mind,
eight billion people. By mid-morning there are no secrets, by noon the whole city moves in step,
by sunset every song in your head is the same song, and at night one fear belongs to everyone.
Would you keep the one mind, or fight for your own?
