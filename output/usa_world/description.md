# What if the United States took over the world?

**Format:** 1080 × 1920 (9:16 vertical) · 60 seconds · 30 fps · H.264 + AAC
**Style:** blocky low poly, street level, one continuous sunrise-to-night cycle

## Short description (for the post)

Every country says yes to the same offer, and a flag with 195 stars goes up over your square.
Kilometers become miles, 20 °C becomes 68 °F, your café charges in dollars with free refills, and
the biggest election in history forms a line that goes around the square twice. Then the catch:
24 time zones, two sports called football, and one tax form for the whole planet. A light, neutral
thought experiment about what "one country" would actually feel like on an ordinary street.
