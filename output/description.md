# What if you used 100% of your brain?

**Format:** 1080 × 1920 (9:16 vertical) · 60 seconds · 30 fps · H.264 + AAC
**Style:** low poly 3D, flat-shaded, one continuous day-to-night cycle

## Short description (for the post)

What would actually happen if you flipped a switch and used 100% of your brain? We follow one
low-poly brain from sunrise to 3 AM: learning every language before breakfast, perfect recall at
noon, an impossible appetite by sunset, and at midnight the truth about what "every neuron firing
at once" really means. Spoiler: you already use all of it.
