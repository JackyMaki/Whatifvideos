# What if you woke up 100 years in the future?

**Format:** 1080 × 1920 (9:16 vertical) · 60 seconds · 30 fps · H.264 + AAC
**Style:** low poly 3D, flat-shaded, one continuous day-to-night cycle

## Short description (for the post)

You fall asleep tonight and open your eyes in 2126. Same bed, very different world. We follow one
low-poly sleeper from sunrise to 2 AM: a town that sinks as a skyline rises, cars in quiet sky
lanes, screens that float in the air, a phone that belongs in a museum, money that no longer
works, everyone you knew gone, and a century of medicine that might let you see 2226. So what
would you do first?
