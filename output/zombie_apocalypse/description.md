## Short description (for the post)

One bite at sunrise in a crowded city. By morning the roads are jammed, by noon the power is out,
by sunset the city belongs to the infected. We follow one survivor from a busy plaza to a rooftop
as the Infected counter climbs from 0% to 95% in a single day, and ask the only question that
matters: where would you go first?
