## Short description (for the post)

An ordinary morning: you step out of your building with a coffee. Across the street, someone bites
a stranger. By mid-morning the roads are jammed and you're climbing your own fire escape with two
neighbors. By noon the power is gone and you're dragging crates against the rooftop door. By night
the city belongs to the infected, a barrel fire is all you have, and one of your neighbors slips
away before dawn. When the food runs out: the rooftop, or the supermarket?
